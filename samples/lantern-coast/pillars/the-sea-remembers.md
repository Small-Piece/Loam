## What players should feel

Watched by something old and patient that knew this coast before the village did.

## What this looks like in play

Tides redraw the shoreline twice a day, opening some paths and drowning others. Creatures such as the [[Tallow Wife]] follow the water, so the tide table matters more than a map. Things lost to the sea wash up again later, changed.

## What this rules out

Random monster spawns, a map that never changes, and enemies that ignore the tide.
