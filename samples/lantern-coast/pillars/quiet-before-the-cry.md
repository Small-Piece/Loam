## What players should feel

Dread that builds in silence and breaks in a single sound.

## What this looks like in play

Most of the time the player hears only wind, surf and their own footsteps. Threats are heard before they are seen: the [[Shorecallers]] imitate gulls, badly. When the cry comes, it means something.

## What this rules out

Constant music, jump scares without a warning sound, and loud ambient filler.
