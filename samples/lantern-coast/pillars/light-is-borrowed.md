## What players should feel

Safe only for as long as the oil lasts. Every pool of light is a small, temporary home.

## What this looks like in play

The keeper's lantern burns oil that has to be found, carried and rationed. Lighthouses can be relit to make lasting safe ground, but each one costs a climb through [[The Drowned Stair]]. Darkness is never a wall; it is a price.

## What this rules out

Permanent safe rooms, pickups that light themselves, and any light source that never runs out.
