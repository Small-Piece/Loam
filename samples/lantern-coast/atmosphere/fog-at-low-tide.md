## Mood

The world shrinks to a few metres. Shapes on the mudflats could be rocks, boats or something waiting.

## Sound

Dripping, a distant bell buoy, the pull of mud under boots. No music.

## Light and colour

Grey-green and low in contrast. The lantern makes a warm bubble that the fog eats away at the edges.

## Pacing of tension

Long, quiet stretches of searching; tension rises as the tide turns and the water starts coming back. See [[The sea remembers]].
