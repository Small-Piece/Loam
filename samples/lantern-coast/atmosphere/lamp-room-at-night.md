## Mood

Relief, and a view. From the top of [[Lantern Point]] the whole coast can be seen for the first time.

## Sound

The hum of the lamp mechanism, wind against the glass, the sea far below.

## Light and colour

The beam sweeps the water in slow white arcs; everything else is deep blue.

## Pacing of tension

A resting place between expeditions. Nothing attacks here; the danger is leaving.
