## Overview

A fishing village of twelve houses on stilts, half of them empty. The people who stayed keep their shutters closed after dusk and leave oil on their doorsteps for the keeper.

## Rules of this world

Nobody goes out on the water after the evening bell. A light in a window means "we are home", never "come in".

## History

The village grew up around [[Lantern Point]]. When the last keeper vanished, the lights along the coast went dark one by one.
