## Overview

The tallest lighthouse on the coast and the keeper's home. Relighting its lamp is the first goal of the game.

## Rules of this world

While its beam turns, creatures stay off the open beach. It needs oil every night.

## History

Built by the first keepers to guide boats home through the fog to [[Saltmarsh Village]].
