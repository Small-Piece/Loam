## Overview

A stairway cut into the cliff, leading from the beach down into sea caves that flood at high tide. It is the only way from the village to the old lighthouses.

## Rules of this world

At high tide the lower steps are under water; anyone below must climb before the sea comes back. The [[Tallow Wife]] waits at the waterline.

## History

Older than the village. The carvings on the walls show lanterns, but no people.
