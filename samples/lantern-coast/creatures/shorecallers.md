## What it is

Long-necked birds that are not quite birds, hunting in pairs across the rooftops.

## How it is sensed before it is seen

A gull's cry that is slightly wrong: too slow, then cut off. See [[Quiet before the cry]].

## What it wants

Anything that moves alone in the dark.

## Rules it follows

They never enter light, and they lose interest in anyone who stands still for a count of ten.
