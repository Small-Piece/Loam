## What it is

A tall, pale figure that drifts at the edge of the water, wrapped in what looks like old candle wax.

## How it is sensed before it is seen

A smell of burnt fat and a slow dripping sound, out of rhythm with the waves.

## What it wants

Light. It follows lanterns and snuffs them out, but never touches the person holding one.

## Rules it follows

It cannot leave the water and moves only while the tide moves. A relit lighthouse keeps it back for one night.
