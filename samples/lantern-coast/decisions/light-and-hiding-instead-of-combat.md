## Decision

The keeper has no weapon. Threats are avoided with light, timing and hiding.

## Why

Combat would turn every creature into a target and make the dark less frightening. Keeping light as the only tool puts [[Light is borrowed]] at the centre of play.

## Alternatives considered

A harpoon for close defence; flares that can be thrown as weapons.

## What would change our mind

Playtesters feeling helpless rather than tense after the first hour.
