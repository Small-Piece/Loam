## Decision

There is no map screen. The keeper reads a tide table, and the coast is learned by walking it.

## Why

It keeps [[The sea remembers]] in every trip: when to leave matters as much as where to go.

## Alternatives considered

A hand-drawn map that fills in as the keeper explores.

## What would change our mind

Playtesters getting lost in a way that feels unfair rather than eerie. Revisited after the second playtest: landmarks were added to the tide table.
