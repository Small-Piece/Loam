## Decision

Interiors use fixed camera angles, framed by hand for each room.

## Why

Framing each room lets the sounds of [[Quiet before the cry]] land at the right moment.

## Alternatives considered

A free camera everywhere.

## What would change our mind

Players losing track of where they are. The first playtest showed exactly that, so this decision was reversed: the camera now follows the keeper everywhere.
